One poster that sums up the world news of **Tuesday 17 November 2026**. A night-side Earth rimmed with sunrise sits at the centre, ringed by six medallions, one per story that dominated the day, each with a short caption:

- **Ukraine strikes Crimea Bridge** — Kyiv's attack partly collapsed the Kerch Bridge; Moscow hit back at Kyiv's bridges.
- **Hormuz Strait: tanker mined, ablaze** — a fourth tanker struck a mine in the strait as the U.S.–Iran war widened at sea.
- **COP31 draft deal: $120bn for climate** — negotiators in Antalya published a draft calling for a faster fossil-fuel phase-out and $120bn a year in adaptation finance.
- **U.S. loses measles-free status** — elimination status gone after 26 years, with a 40-year high in cases.
- **Xi–Trump–Putin: summit set, Shenzhen** — the first trilateral summit of the three leaders was announced for Thursday at APEC.
- **Nvidia chip spy scandal** — the chip giant's chief resigned amid the espionage scandal as AI stocks slid.

The artwork ships as `entry.png` (a 1024×1024 JPEG, 305,817 bytes). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) copies those bytes verbatim to the output, so two runs produce byte-identical files. Two alternate finished candidates (`candidate-2.jpg`, `candidate-3.jpg`) are kept in the repository; the shipped picture is `candidate-1.jpg`, and swapping it is a one-line change in the build notes.

## Run

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Output: `out/entry.png`, identical bytes on every run.
